Contact

Email marshbarbrv@gmail.com. We read every message and aim to reply within 24 hours.

FAQ

Who can use Kandi? You must be 18 or older. When you create an account you agree to the Terms of Use and confirm your age.

Is Kandi free? Yes. This version is free to use. Paid plans, when they are offered in the iOS app, will use Apple In-App Purchase only. We will not ask you to pay outside the App Store in the iOS app.

How do likes, matches, and chat work? Like someone’s photo or prompt. If they like you back, you match and can message each other.

What are demo profiles? Some accounts are labeled as demo characters so you can try the app. They are not real people.

How do events and meetups work? Open an event, tap I’m going or Open to meet, and see who’s going. Meetup notes are typed in the app — Kandi does not share your live GPS location.

Safety, reporting, and blocking

You can report or block someone from their profile, and report a post from the post menu. Reporting also blocks that person. Their profile, posts, and messages no longer appear in your app, and the match is removed.

Kandi has zero tolerance for harassment, hate, sexual content involving minors, and other objectionable content. We review reports and remove violating content or accounts within 24 hours.

How to delete your account

In the app, open the You tab, go to Settings, and tap Delete account. Type your username and the word DELETE in all caps to confirm. This permanently removes your profile, photos, posts, likes, matches, and messages.

If you cannot use the in-app control, email marshbarbrv@gmail.com from a note that identifies your username and we will delete the account for you.

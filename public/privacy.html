Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026. Kandi is an 18+ app for meeting friends and dates in the rave and festival scene.

What we collect

When you create an account we store the information you give us: username, password (stored as a hash, not in plain text), name, age, date of birth if you add it, home city, bio, photos and short videos you upload, vibe and “looking for” tags, gender, pronouns, height, hobbies, music taste, song links you add (for example Spotify, SoundCloud, or Apple Music), optional Instagram, Facebook, or Spotify profile links, top artists you pick (names and artwork looked up through Apple's iTunes Search API), prompt answers, events you mark yourself as going to, and optional in-app meetup notes. We also store activity needed to run the app: posts, comments, likes, follows, passes, matches, messages (including photos, videos, and voice notes you send in chat), crews you join, stories, blocks, reports, and a session cookie so you stay signed in.

If you choose to get verified, we collect a live selfie of you holding a pose we pick. That selfie is stored privately, used only to compare it with your profile photos when we review your request, and is never shown on your public profile or to other members. We delete it when you delete your account.

We do not collect your email address when you sign up. Meetup notes are typed in the app and are not GPS, SMS, or live location. We do not use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not sell your personal information.

How we use it

We use this information to show your profile to other members, power Discover, likes, matches, chat, the Home feed, events, and “at the show” features, enforce the 18+ age gate, rank more active members fairly in feeds, handle reports and blocks, and keep the service secure. Demo profiles are example characters, not real people.

Who can see your profile

Other signed-in members can see the profile information you publish (name, age, photos, prompts, and similar). People you block cannot see you, and you will not see them. Messages are only visible to the two people in a match.

Retention and deletion

You can delete your account anytime in the app: open the You tab, choose Delete account, then type your username and DELETE to confirm. That permanently removes your profile, photos, verification selfies, likes, matches, messages, and related account data. Reports may be kept in a limited form if needed for safety. Demo profiles cannot be deleted.

Cookies

We use an HTTP-only session cookie (kandi_sid) to keep you signed in. It is not used for advertising.

Children

Kandi is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions or a deletion request if you cannot use the in-app control, email marshbarbrv@gmail.com or visit Support.
